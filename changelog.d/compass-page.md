## en
### Added
- **Investment compass page** in the Research group: which way to lean now between property, Vietnam stocks, world stocks, gold, crypto, savings and USD, with a cycle clock for the world and Vietnam.
- Each asset has buy-when and sell-when signals; the measurable ones are checked against live numbers from the Markets page (VN-Index, gold, the dollar, US yields, Bitcoin, the fear index).
- A grid of what moves each asset (rates, oil, war, tariffs, foreign flows, sentiment...), suggested splits for Cautious, Balanced and Growth investors, an event calendar and if-then scenarios.
- An **Update outlook** button and a monthly schedule: Javis researches the new numbers and writes a draft; you preview it and press Approve before it applies, and the old version is kept.
