## en
### Added
- **Markets page** in a new **Research** group: Vietnam stocks (VN-Index, VN30, HNX, UPCoM, ~75 large caps by sector), world indices, gold, oil, FX, yields and the top 100 crypto. Live data from public sources, no API key, refreshed every 3 minutes.
- Full charts: market-state gauges, heatmaps, sector bars, the Fear & Greed index, sortable tables and a detailed candlestick chart with moving averages, Bollinger bands, volume, RSI and MACD.
- **Outlook tab**: an automatic read-out, an opportunity map, and lists of promising symbols, symbols set for a big move, risks and possible rebounds, with an expected 5-session range. These are technical signals, not investment advice.
- Ask Javis about the markets in chat (the `javis_market` tool for every brain) and add your own symbols to a watchlist.
